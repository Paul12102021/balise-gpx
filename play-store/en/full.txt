Pisteo guides you on the trail, on foot or by bike. Free, no account, no ads.

FOLLOW YOUR TRACK — Open a GPX file or a route link: Pisteo guides you by voice at every junction, even with the screen off, and brings you back on track or to the start.

FIND A RIDE OR HIKE — A loop of the length you want, a route to a place, a point-by-point route, or waymarked and recommended trails near you.

PLAN IT — Weather along the route for your start time, headwind, nightfall warning, lit streets at night, elevation profile, water points, huts, shelters and summits.

GO WITH PEACE OF MIND — Your loved ones follow your position live with a simple link, no app needed. Distance left and the next turn show on the lock screen. Multi-day trips are split into stages, and Pisteo offers today's stage each morning.

THE RIGHT MAPS — Official maps for France (IGN), Spain, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium, the UK, Canada and the US, aerial photos, and offline maps.

KEEP YOUR OUTINGS — GPS recording, monthly summary and records, photos placed on the route, upload to Strava, backup to your Google Drive, and route sharing by link or QR code.
